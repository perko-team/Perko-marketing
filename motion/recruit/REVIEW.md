Concept 6 · Wow 6 · Composition 5 · Motion 6 · Variety 6 · Look 5 · Craft 5 · Brand/message 6 · Sound n/a · Comprehension 5. Ship: no.

Weakest: the street world from 2 to 8 s (a featureless dive over asphalt, a greybox city, hoop signs that read as bicycle wheels, a "wave" that reads as a laser line) — where the device, the look and the business message all fall apart.
Other notes: hook line grey on entry; street names and "free" too small; the business line clipped at the frame edge; the card line is a consumer pitch, not recruitment; the fold doesn't read; the cut into the end card is unmotivated; the end card's lower third is empty.

Changes for round 2:
- The dive follows the chip down (no dead asphalt); the chip lands on the drop and turns into a stamp; the wave is a circle spreading from it, and each sign lights when the wave reaches it (cause and effect).
- Signs are blank paper discs on brackets (unstamped stamps) that turn lime; facades get a sign band and upper windows; buildings fold flat on hinges.
- The business line lands on the drop over the sky, fitted to the width, with the street names large; the card line becomes "יש מקום גם לעסק שלך."; "free" grows; the camera pushes into an open slot into the end card; the end card gets the ten-slot row.
