# Treatment: "Through the punch hole"

**Idea.** The paper punch card every neighbourhood business already uses is the door: a hole punched in it opens onto the street, and the street is full of open places waiting for their first stamp.

**Core device.** The punched hole. It is cut on the second beat, the camera follows the chip through it down to the street, and the chip, landed on the road, sends the wave that lights the open spots. Every later beat is a consequence of that hole.

**Tone.** Punchy and warm. Bold camera, calm type.

**Material language.**
- Card stock: off-white paper with fibres (the punch card, the ground, the facades), lit by a warm key from the upper left with soft shadows.
- Brand card stock: lime and ink facades and awnings, an ink road with a paper centre line.
- Lime light (custom shader `litRingLook`): the only thing that glows. A light that runs around each lit ring and kicks on every beat; the punched hole's rim; the wave on the road.

**Motif.** The circle: the stamp on the card, the punched hole, the chip, the shop-sign rings, the slots on the Perko card, the "o" of the wordmark.

**Music (placeholder).** An original score in code, 120 BPM (MUSIC-MAP.md): a paper clack at 0.5 s, a whoosh through the hole, the peak at 4.0 s (bar 2), the groove to 12.0 s, the end card on the stop. The user will supply the final track; the edit will be re-timed to its map.
